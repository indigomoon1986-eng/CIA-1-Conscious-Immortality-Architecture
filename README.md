## Central Proposition

Conscious immortality should be investigated as the preservation of an ongoing, causally connected subjective process, rather than the reproduction of memories, behavior, or personality alone.

A machine could reproduce a person’s speech, remember their experiences, and convincingly imitate their decisions without demonstrating that the original person’s subjective experience had continued.

CIA-1 formulates the difference between **replication** and **continuation**.
